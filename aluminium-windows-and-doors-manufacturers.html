How to Compare Aluminium Windows and Doors Manufacturers

When evaluating aluminium windows and doors manufacturers, compare more than the visible frame. Ask about the proposed system, profile depth, glass compatibility, hardware, drainage, weather sealing, fabrication tolerances, installation method and support after completion.

The right configuration changes with the opening. A compact bedroom window, a broad balcony slider and a commercial façade do not have identical structural or operational requirements. A dependable manufacturer should explain why a particular sliding, fixed, casement or door system is appropriate rather than recommending one standard option everywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I check before choosing aluminium windows?

Consider opening size, building height, wind and rain exposure, ventilation needs, glass type, mesh, hardware, drainage, finish and installation conditions. The complete system should be selected around the site.

Are aluminium windows suitable for large openings?

Aluminium systems are often considered for wider openings because suitable profiles can provide slim sightlines and structural strength. Exact suitability depends on the system, glass weight, panel size and engineering requirements.

Which aluminium window opening styles are available?

Common options include fixed, sliding, casement, openable and combination windows. Door options may include sliding, French, casement and larger premium slider configurations.

Does FabFrame provide customised aluminium windows and doors?

FabFrame offers made-to-measure system aluminium configurations. Final options can be discussed according to site measurements, opening style, glass, finish, mesh and hardware requirements.
